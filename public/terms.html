  • These terms may change at any time. Using Dash after a change means you accept the new version.

Privacy

  • What's stored: a Google sign-in token, a copy of your Trello board and list names, your last 10 chat messages, a list of recently created items (for undo), an activity log and usage totals. Everything is stored in Cloudflare KV in the owner's Cloudflare account.
  • What's sent elsewhere: your messages, along with your Trello list names, go to Anthropic so its AI can understand them. Voice recordings go to Cloudflare Workers AI to be transcribed and aren't kept.
  • Google data: Dash only asks for access to calendar events. It uses that access solely to create, read and delete events when you ask it to. Your Google data is never sold, shared, or used for ads or AI training. Dash's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Removing your data: use Clear chat and Clear activity in the menu. To revoke Google access, visit your Google account permissions. To revoke Trello access, go to your Trello account settings under Applications.
  • No cookies are used except one that keeps you logged in.
